End-to-End Workflow

How PROCUREMENTIQ AI Works

A seamless, auditable 5-step process designed for high-value enterprise procurement teams.

1

Create Request

Specify equipment name, quantity, budget ceiling, delivery location, and mandatory warranty requirements.

2

Upload Quotes

Drop supplier quotations in PDF, XLSX, or CSV formats. Parsers normalize unit prices, freight, and terms.

3

Analyze Risks

Evaluate historical on-time rates, compliance certificates, warranty deficits, and delivery timeline buffers.

4

Optimize Costs

Calculate transparent total cost of ownership including discounts, regional taxes, freight, and installation.

5

Shortlist & Decide

Review ranked recommendations with clear trade-offs, negotiation tips, and downloadable PDF/CSV reports.

Multi-Agent Intelligence

Six Specialized Autonomous Agents

Orchestrated with LangGraph and local deterministic fallbacks for 100% offline accuracy.

AGENT 01

Requirement Parser

Converts raw procurement requests into structured mandatory constraints and preferred criteria.

Inputs: RFP Specs Outputs: Constraint Gates
AGENT 02

Quote Analyzer

Extracts and normalizes multi-format quotation documents, surfacing missing fields for human confirmation.

Inputs: PDF/XLSX/CSV Outputs: Normalized Bids
AGENT 03

Supplier Researcher

Gathers verified vendor reliability profiles, historical on-time delivery rates, defect history, and certifications.

Inputs: Vendor IDs Outputs: Verified Profiles
AGENT 04

Risk Assessor

Evaluates delivery risk, compliance evidence, warranty limitations, and unusual commercial terms deterministically.

Inputs: Bids & Evidence Outputs: 0-100 Risk Scores
AGENT 05

Cost Optimizer

Calculates landed total costs (subtotal, discounts, freight, installation, taxes) separating arithmetic from narrative.

Inputs: Pricing Rows Outputs: Landed Costs
AGENT 06

Procurement Advisor

Synthesizes all evidence into an explainable ranked shortlist, highlighting the recommended vendor and negotiation tactics.

Inputs: 5 Agent Outputs Outputs: Explainable Shortlist
